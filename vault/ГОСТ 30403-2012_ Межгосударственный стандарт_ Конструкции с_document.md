**Section 1: Область применения (Area of application)**
- The current standard establishes a method of testing construction structures without openings for fire safety.
- The current standard applies to elements of buildings - columns, girders, trusses, beams, arches, frames and connections, stairs and floor slabs of staircases and stairs, external and internal walls, partitions, coverings, roofs, staircases, floors, fireproofing, fire-resistant barriers.
- The current standard is allowed to be applied for comparative assessment of fire safety of suspended ceilings, air ducts and ventilation systems.
- The current standard does not apply to construction of filling openings, floor coverings, roofing, external walls with completed on their outer surface systems of external thermal insulation, cladding and finishing, as well as protective-decorative systems, designed for application on two and more types of walls.
